Introduction

Welcome to QuickChat, your ultimate WhatsApp companion. Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we handle your information when you use our app. We are committed to transparency and protecting your personal data.

Information We Collect

QuickChat follows a privacy-first approach and collects minimal information:

  • Phone Numbers: When you enter a phone number to open it in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.
  • Contact Information: When you voluntarily save phone numbers within the app's contact manager.
  • Message Templates: When you create and save message templates within the app.
  • Usage Data: App activity logs stored locally for your reference (call history, timestamps).
  • App Preferences: Your settings such as lock screen preferences, theme choices, and display options.
  • Google Account (optional): If you choose to sign in with Google as a premium subscriber, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo solely to identify your account for cloud sync.

What We Don't Collect

  • Your personal messages or chat content
  • Contact lists from your phone's native contacts
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Payment or billing information

How We Use Your Information

All collected information serves specific purposes within the app:

  • To enable direct WhatsApp access without saving numbers to device contacts
  • To maintain your personal contact list and message templates within the app
  • To provide WhatsApp Web QR code scanning functionality
  • To display your recent activity history for easy reference
  • To implement security features like app locking
  • To sync your contacts and templates to the cloud if you have an active premium subscription and are signed in with Google
  • To verify your subscription status and unlock premium features
  • To improve app performance and user experience

Cloud Storage & Sync Optional

☁️ How Cloud Sync Works

Cloud storage is completely optional and only available to active premium subscribers. You can use the full app without ever signing in or enabling cloud sync.

  • Cloud sync requires signing in with your Google account.
  • Your contacts are stored in Firebase Firestore under the path quickchat_contacts/{userId}/contacts.
  • Your message templates are stored under quickchat_templates/{userId}/templates.
  • Firebase security rules ensure only you can read or write your own data.
  • All communication with Firebase is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • You can sign out at any time to stop cloud sync. Your local data remains on your device.
  • You can permanently delete all cloud data at any time from Account Settings → Delete Account Data inside the app.

We use Firebase (by Google) for authentication and cloud storage. Firebase's privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Data Storage and Security Local First

Local Storage (Default)

All your data is stored exclusively on your device by default. We do not transmit any information to external servers unless you explicitly enable cloud sync as a premium subscriber.

Security Measures

  • Device Encryption: Data is stored using your device's native encryption
  • Lock Screen Protection: Optional PIN, pattern, or biometric authentication
  • Firebase Security Rules: Cloud data is protected so only you can access it
  • HTTPS Encryption: All cloud communications are fully encrypted
  • Secure Deletion: Complete local data removal upon app uninstallation
  • Cloud Data Deletion: Delete your cloud data any time from within the app

Advertising

The free version of QuickChat displays advertisements served by Google AdMob and may include mediation through Unity Ads. These services may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant advertisements. Premium subscribers have all advertisements permanently removed.

For more information about how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob, visit policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-Party Services

Our app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play: For app distribution and subscription billing
  • Firebase (Google): For optional authentication and cloud data storage
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management and verification
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (free users only)
  • Unity Ads: For ad mediation (free users only)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: For optional push notifications
  • WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business: External apps opened by user action only

Sharing Your Information

We do not share, sell, trade, or rent your personal information to anyone.

The only exception is when you explicitly choose to share contacts or messages through your device's sharing capabilities (WhatsApp, email, messaging apps, etc.). This sharing is controlled entirely by you.

Your Rights and Controls

You have complete control over your data:

  • Full Access: View, edit, or delete any contact or template you've saved
  • History Management: Clear your recent activity logs at any time
  • Cloud Opt-Out: Sign out of Google at any time to stop cloud sync
  • Cloud Data Deletion: Permanently delete all cloud data from Account Settings in the app
  • Google Permission Revocation: Revoke app access at myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Privacy Settings: Enable/disable app locking and security features
  • Data Export: Share your saved contacts via standard sharing options
  • Complete Removal: Uninstall the app to delete all local data
